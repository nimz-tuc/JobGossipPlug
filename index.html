How JobGossipPlug earns money

JobGossipPlug is a free guide to opportunities with third-party companies. We do not employ you, we are not these companies, and we cannot promise you work, pay or an acceptance. The company decides who it accepts and what it pays.

Some Apply buttons use a referral link. If you are accepted through that link, the company may pay JobGossipPlug a reward. It does not change what you are paid and it does not cost you anything. Every opportunity that uses such a link is labelled Referral link.

What we collect

Your name, email, country, whether you are starting out or skilled, and the fields you pick. We use your country and experience only to show you opportunities that suit you. We also count how often each Apply button is pressed. We do not sell your data, and we only share the web address you click with the company when you press Apply.

Your choices

You can change your details any time on My profile. To delete your account, contact the site owner.

Always read a company's own terms before you apply. Country rules and requirements can change.
